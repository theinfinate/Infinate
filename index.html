Rec song adds every pattern you switch to onto the end of the song as you play. Tap a transition, then a pattern: it plays into that switch once.

Drag a block's right edge to make it longer or shorter, and its left edge to change where in the pattern it starts (both snap to quarters). Drag the middle to move it; tap to select it. Transitions sit in the lane above: drag them, drag their ends, tap one to change it, or tap an empty spot to add one.

Tap + after a block to add a transition into the next one. The song loops back to the start.

        EQ & compressor

        Looper ·

        On the pads: tap a looper pad to record, tap again to finish. The length snaps to whole bars, and the first loop sets the tempo if nothing is playing.
        Rec → Play: taps then stop and start the loop. Rec → Dub → Play: taps go overdub, play, overdub… Rec → Play ⇄ Dub: taps switch between play and overdub, and a double tap stops the loop.
        Hold half a second to undo the last layer (hold again to redo). Keep holding for two seconds to clear the loop; the pad flashes as it clears.
